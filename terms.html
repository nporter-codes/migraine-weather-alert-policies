2. Purpose and limits

The app supports regional weather awareness and personal symptom records. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, predict whether you will have a migraine, or establish that weather caused a symptom. Personal comparisons describe the records you entered; missing days are not proof that you had no symptoms.

Do not use forecasts, comparisons or alerts for emergency or medication decisions. Discuss care decisions with a qualified clinician.

Forecasts may change, be incomplete or differ from conditions at your precise location. Notifications, widgets and Watch updates depend on connectivity, permissions, operating-system scheduling and provider availability. Delivery or timing is not guaranteed. An old or unavailable forecast is not a reassuring weather result.

3. Free features and Premium

Version 2.0 free access includes 48-hour pressure forecasts, basic pressure reminders, quick records, recent 30-day journal browsing, export of all raw records as CSV, and deletion controls.

Premium adds a seven-day daily outlook, personal comparisons when sufficient data is available, complete history browsing, PDF reports and optional read-only Apple Health sleep import. Monthly and annual plans unlock the same Premium features. Older records remain stored when Premium ends; the browsing entitlement changes.

Features can be temporarily unavailable due to outages, incomplete data or service capacity, including free-service limits. Paying for Premium does not guarantee a notification, complete forecast coverage or provider availability.
