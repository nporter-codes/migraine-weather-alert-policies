Support

A little help, when you need it.

Start with the steps below, or email lucktuite@outlook.com. No app account is required.

These instructions describe the upcoming version 2.0 update. Screen names and features in earlier versions may differ. Check the installed app version when contacting support.

Setup and weather

Can I use the app without current-location access?

Yes. Choose a city in the location picker. The weather backend receives a rounded 0.1-degree region. Current-location access is optional, and the app does not send continuous precise GPS tracking.

Why is a forecast unavailable or old?

Check your connection, open the iPhone app and refresh. Check the provider’s issue time and any old-forecast notice. An unavailable or old forecast is not a low-change result. You can still record symptoms offline. Premium does not fix an outage or exhausted service capacity.

Why did I not receive an alert?

Open Weather alerts and review connection status, selected region, direction, sensitivity and quiet hours. Check notification permission and Focus in iOS Settings. There may be no qualifying pressure window, a daily limit may have been reached, or delivery may be delayed. A scheduled or provider-accepted message does not guarantee device delivery.

Watch and widget

Why do the Watch and widget show an older forecast?

They use cached weather information and its update time. iOS/watchOS determine refresh opportunities; connectivity can delay updates. Open the paired iPhone app for the latest available forecast. The widget snapshot contains no journal or Health sleep records.

What does “waiting to sync” mean on the Watch?

The quick record is queued on the Watch until the paired iPhone acknowledges receipt. Keep both devices available, reconnect them and open the iPhone app. Check for the record on the phone before assuming it arrived. A waiting state is not an acknowledgement. The companion does not independently receive a separate weather push stream; iOS decides whether a notification is presented on the phone or Watch.

iCloud, Health and offline records

How do I enable or retry iCloud sync?

In Settings → Data & privacy, enable iCloud sync to sync journal records through your private Apple account database. Imported Apple Health sleep is excluded. Check that your device has an available iCloud account and connection, then use Sync now. If sync fails, the app keeps local entries and reports the problem. Turning sync off stops further app sync but does not erase previously synced copies; a request already submitted may finish. Network recovery alone does not guarantee an immediate background retry.

Why does sleep show no data?

Sleep import is optional, read-only and requires Premium. Completing a permission request does not prove readable sleep data exists. Check for recorded sleep in the queried period and review the app’s access in Apple Health. Imported sleep stays on this device. Disconnect sleep to clear the current imported display; original Apple Health records remain in Health.

What can I do offline?

You can create local journal records. Forecasts may be cached or unavailable, Watch records can remain queued, and iCloud, cloud deletion, notifications and purchase/restore checks can require a connection. Read the status shown in the app and retry the relevant action when online.

Subscriptions

What is free and what needs Premium?

Free features include the 48-hour pressure forecast, basic pressure reminders, quick records, recent 30-day journal browsing, export of all raw records as CSV and deletion. Premium adds the seven-day daily outlook, personal comparisons, full history browsing, PDF reports and optional Apple Health sleep import. Ending Premium does not delete older records.

How do trial, restore and cancellation work?

Monthly and annual plans unlock the same features. The monthly plan may offer a three-day free trial and the annual plan a seven-day free trial, subject to Apple-confirmed eligibility and offer availability. Only one introductory offer is available per subscription group; trials cannot be combined. The Apple purchase sheet shows the actual regional price and terms. Use Restore purchases in Premium with the original Apple account; review the result shown. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting app data or uninstalling does not cancel. See Apple’s cancellation help, refund help and our terms.

Export and deletion

How do I export records?

Open Settings → Data & privacy → Export my records. Complete raw CSV export does not require Premium. PDF reports require Premium. Choose where to save or share the file; copies you save outside the app are controlled separately.

Which deletion option should I choose?

In Data & privacy, device-and-selected-iCloud deletion removes the app data on this device and the selected current cloud journal where applicable. Older, unselected iCloud copies are retained and blocked from reimport on that device. Device-and-all-iCloud-copies deletion also requests removal of older app cloud copies. Read the confirmation and export anything you want to keep first.

Deletion also requests applicable backend deregistration and Firebase token/installation cleanup. A minimal backend replay-protection marker remains for 30 days; bounded maintenance and provider backup processes can extend physical removal. See the privacy policy.

What if deletion fails or remains pending?

Keep the app installed so the saved request and credentials remain available. Reconnect and retry from Data & privacy. The app retains the request’s scope for retry. Do not treat a pending state or uninstall as confirmation of cloud deletion. Contact support if retry still fails, without sending journal contents or credentials. Apple Health source records, your subscription and exported copies are separate.

Contact support or make a privacy request

Email lucktuite@outlook.com with only the minimum details needed: app version, iOS/watchOS version if relevant, the screen/action and a brief description. For privacy requests, describe what you want to access, correct or delete; we may ask for limited verification information.

Do not send health notes, passwords, purchase receipts, push tokens or installation credentials. Do not post health records or device identifiers in this public repository. This site has no contact form or journal upload.
